Frequently asked questions

Helpful answers.

How do I find an opportunity?

Visit the Opportunities page and browse by category, location, or opportunity type.

Who can post an opportunity?

University departments, facilities, organizations, businesses, individuals, and other trusted providers can share opportunities.

Is CampusLink only for students?

No. CampusLink is for students and people within and around the university environment.

Can businesses post opportunities?

Yes. Local and campus-facing businesses can post jobs, internships, part-time work, and useful services.
