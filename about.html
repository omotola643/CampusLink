About CampusLink

Making campus opportunities easier to find.

CampusLink is a web-based opportunity platform for students and people within the university environment. It brings jobs, internships, part-time work, and services into one clear space instead of leaving them scattered across group chats, social media, notice boards, and word of mouth.

Students, departments, facilities, businesses, organizations, and individuals can use CampusLink to discover or share opportunities that fit the realities of campus life.
